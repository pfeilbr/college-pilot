💔 Divorced parents: the rule that changes everything

Since the 2024 FAFSA overhaul, the FAFSA counts one parent: the one who provides the most financial support (usually the parent the student lives with). The other parent's income is invisible to it.

  • FAFSA ONLY Delaware, UMass, Penn State & Pitt — aid is computed on the one FAFSA household. A moderate custodial income reads as high need here no matter what the other parent earns.
  • CSS + BOTH PARENTS Villanova, Fordham, Northeastern, BU, Tufts — these use the CSS Profile with noncustodial-parent information. Both households' income and assets count toward institutional aid. If the other parent earns well, the generous "meets 100% of need" pledges shrink fast.
  • Waivers exist but are strict: CSS noncustodial waivers are for no-contact/abandonment situations — not for "he won't pay." Divorce decrees saying who pays for college don't bind the aid formulas.

🐘 Pennsylvania money that travels (or doesn't)

  • PA State Grant (PHEAA): income-based, up to ~$5,750/yr — works at all PA schools (Villanova, Penn State, Pitt) and is portable to Delaware and Massachusetts (UD, UMass, Northeastern, BU, Tufts) but NOT to New York — Fordham can't use it. A moderate-income household often qualifies for a partial grant.
  • PA 529 plans spend anywhere, with a PA state-tax deduction on contributions.
  • American Opportunity Tax Credit: up to $2,500/yr back at tax time for tuition payers under the income caps.
  • Federal baseline everyone gets: $5,500 first-year federal loan (rising yearly), plus work-study eligibility where need exists.

🎯 The strategy in one paragraph

For a family where the FAFSA parent earns a moderate income: if the combined-parent picture is also moderate, the CSS need-met schools (BU, Northeastern, Tufts — and Villanova, whose real net at moderate income is ~$21K) can drop below the publics. If the noncustodial household earns a lot, those schools jump toward sticker and the winners become the FAFSA-only four — with Pitt as the affordability anchor (~$38K sticker, ~$23K net at moderate income, direct-admit business) and Delaware/UMass adding automatic merit. Penn State's aid is famously thin — its sticker barely bends. Run the NPCs both ways before ranking anything.

🗓️ Aid deadlines to calendar

  • Junior year (2026–27): run every NPC twice — once with custodial income only, once combined — before building the final list
  • Oct 1, 2027: CSS Profile opens (needed for the four privates; ~$25/school, fee waivers exist)
  • Fall 2027: FAFSA opens — file early; PA State Grant deadline typically May 1 for fall
  • With each ED/EA deadline: aid forms are due at or near the application date — late forms mean late (worse) packages
  • Spring 2028: compare official offers line-by-line; appeal with competing offers where reasonable

How to use this app

📱 Install it

Add to your home screen (☰ menu → "Add to home screen") — it installs as an app, works offline once loaded, and each school guide has one-tap directions and admissions contacts for the visit day.

🔄 Keep it fresh

The ☰ menu's Update app button pulls the latest version instantly. The version stamp at the bottom of the menu tells you what you're running.

⚖️ Trust but verify

Every number is sourced (see each guide's Sources section) and Reddit quotes are real archived posts — but data changes annually and anecdotes are anecdotes. Confirm anything decision-critical with the school.
